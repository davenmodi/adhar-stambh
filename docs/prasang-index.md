# Prasang index: *When the World Sleeps*

Source book: **When the World Sleeps: A Book About Mahant Swami Maharaj at Night** by Sadhu Saumyavadandas (Swaminarayan Aksharpith, 1st edition 2026, ISBN 978-81-988674-5-2).

The book is copyrighted ("All rights reserved"), so the PDF is **not** stored in this public repo. Keep the PDF in a private place, such as a shared drive. This page is only an index for finding prasangs: the summaries are short and in our own words. Before anything goes on a wall, take the exact wording from the book and get it approved.

Page numbers are the PDF page numbers. The book runs through one night, from 9 p.m. to 4 a.m., in four parts: Nightfall, Midnight, Dreams and Birdsong.

## Other night prasangs, by theme

**Letters for people, late at night**
- 09:40 PM, 3 Jan 2018, Surat (p. 24): asks for letters at bedtime, even though there are none on hand.
- 09:50 PM, 1 Apr 2022, Surat (p. 32): has a cold and a full day behind him, and still insists on writing letters.
- 10:00 PM, 26 Jan 2020, Navsari (p. 38): yawning, but keeps writing.
- 11:25 PM, 28 Sep 2015, UK (p. 55): replies at length to a youth report that needed no reply.
- 02:30 AM, 8 Mar 2024, Ahmedabad (p. 135) and the Introduction (p. 9): the 1973 letter written at 2:30 a.m. in Khajurdi, in the dark so as not to wake anyone.
- 03:50 AM, 18 May 2015, Wellington (p. 163): writes a farewell letter to his host before sunrise.

**Seva and humility**

- 02:45 AM, 21 Feb 2020, Anand (p. 143): remaking his own bed until the last crease is gone.
- 01:00 AM, 1970s, Mumbai (p. 80): checking on young sadhus on cold nights.

**Shastras and reading at night**
- 10:08 PM, Aug 2019, Dar es Salaam (p. 39): "Rest? What rest?" and keeps reading.
